Case Study
LIGHTPIC
Designing an AI-Powered Event Photo Platform with Face Recognition
Overview

LightPic is an AI-powered event photo platform that helps photographers and event organizers deliver photos instantly using facial recognition. The platform was designed to simplify photo discovery, automate album organization, and provide a seamless experience for both clients and guests.

We built a modern, performance-focused website that clearly communicates the product while delivering a smooth experience across desktop and mobile devices.

The Challenge
Present a complex AI product in a simple, user-friendly way
Build credibility for a modern SaaS platform
Design a responsive marketing website
Optimize for search engines and performance
Create clear conversion paths for sign-ups and demos
Our Solution

We designed and developed a clean, modern website that combines premium visuals with an intuitive user experience while implementing a strong technical SEO foundation.
